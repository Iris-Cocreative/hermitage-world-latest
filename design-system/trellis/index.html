.hw-timeline · about/

Our story

From a retreat house in Indianapolis, to seven wooded acres on Fall Creek, to a worldwide community that meets every week.

Friar Justin’s life

  1. 1979

    Alverna Retreat House

    Friar Justin begins teaching Success: Full Living and meditation in Indianapolis.

  2. 1985

    The Franciscan Hermitage

    With a few volunteers and supporters, he founds the Hermitage as an independent not-for-profit, soon at home on Fall Creek.

  3. 2023

    Justin’s passing

    Friar Justin dies on October 13, at 88, shortly after a final retreat to Assisi.

  4. 2026

    Hermitage World

    The building is released; the Hermitage meets at the Indiana Interchurch Center and online everywhere. hermitageindy.org becomes hermitageworld.org.

.hw-pathway · programs/

A path that turns, not a ladder

  1. Discover

    Short teachings, the Justin archive, articles and the free healing meditations.

  2. Experience

    Meditate with us online, introductory evenings, open celebrations and livestreams.

  3. Explore

    One-off workshops and talks with Hermitage teachers and guests.

  4. Practice

    Four- to eight-week programs: Full Living, Train Your Mind, book groups.

  5. Belong

    Weekly groups, The Way Between, shared meals and the online community.

  6. Deepen

    Day and weekend retreats, advanced programs and spiritual formation.

  7. Serve

    Facilitate circles, mentor newcomers, volunteer, and help carry the tradition forward.

.hw-pathway-list · meditation/

  1. Settle

    Sit upright and at ease, in a chair or on a cushion. Let your hands rest. Close your eyes or let them soften.

  2. Relax

    Breathe a little slower than usual. Let the body loosen from the top of the head down to the feet.

  3. Rest

    Let your attention rest on the breath, or on one quiet word. When thoughts arrive, notice them without argument.

  4. Return

    Come back to the breath as often as you need to. Returning is the practice. Close with a moment of thanks.
