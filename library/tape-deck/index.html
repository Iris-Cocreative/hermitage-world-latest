A study for the library

Play it as a tape

The meditations were first recorded and sold on tape. In the library, each volume plays as the tape it was: the reels turn, the tape winds across, and the counter keeps time.

How it would work

Each Opus CD holds two of the original volumes. The library now keeps every volume on its own, drawn as a cassette on the shelf, and the CD stays as the album that holds them.

Opening a volume you own puts it in the deck, with its meditations as the tracks on side A. A single meditation plays the same way, on its own.

The sound here is a sample bowl, made in the page, while the Hermitage's recordings are being prepared.
