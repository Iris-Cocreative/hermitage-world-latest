About the Hermitage

Is the Hermitage religious?

It is spiritual and interfaith. It grew from the Franciscan tradition of its founder, Friar Justin Belitz, and it honors every tradition and none. Nothing here asks for belief; everything invites practice.

Where is the Hermitage now?

Online, every week, from anywhere; and in person in Indianapolis at the Indiana Interchurch Center, 1100 West 42nd Street. Visiting

What happened to the building?

In September 2026 the Hermitage left its longtime home and moved to the Interchurch Center. It is not an ending. Justin always said the hermitage is a quiet space within each person, and that goes wherever we do.

Is this the same as hermitageindy.org?

Yes. Hermitage World is the same community under a new name, now that it reaches far beyond Indianapolis. The old address will lead here.

Meditation

Do I need any experience?

None. Start with a first sitting, come to a gathering, or take Train Your Mind.

What kind of meditation is taught here?

Two complementary kinds, as Justin taught them: active meditation, which gives thought and imagination a direction, and contemplation, which rests in the quiet with no agenda at all. More on meditation

Are the meditations free?

The healing meditations are, always. Other recordings carry a nominal cost that helps keep the Hermitage going.

Programs & gatherings

Do gatherings cost anything?

No. The weekly gatherings are free and always optional.

How do I register for a program?

Registration opens on each program’s page when its dates are set. Leave your email there and we’ll write when it opens. pricing to come

I arrived after a gathering started. Can I still join?

Yes. A gathering shows as happening now until it is over, and you are welcome to slip in quietly.
