# Trellis ledger: Hermitage World

What the Hermitage needed that Trellis didn't give, what we did instead, and what Trellis might gain. Entries are how Trellis grows (see the `trellis` skill).

| Date | Trellis | Needed | Did instead | Trellis could |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-13 | 0.9 | A link drawn as a button without an underline | `a.button { text-decoration: none }` in the app's `site-trellis.css` (and the draft's `home.css`) | Add `a.button` beside `a.tab, a.tag` in core's link reset (still open in 0.10) |
| 2026-11-13 | 0.9 | An SVG symbol sheet that takes no space | `.hw-sprite` (absolute, 0 × 0) | Leave hidden `svg` that holds only `symbol` out of the reset that makes every `svg` a block |
| 2026-11-19 | 0.10 | A dark band on a light page (the bar, the map, the footer) | The draft's rooms set `color-scheme` only; since 0.10 every role is worked out on `:root` and on each scope, so a band is now `data-product="hermitage" data-theme="dark"` (a scope with a mood) | Nothing: this is the documented way (D96). Noted because the draft's rooms silently stopped recoloring |
| 2026-11-19 | 0.10 | A success color with 3:1 on the deepest raised ground | Core derives `--ok` at the accent's lightness; with gold at L .625 it was 2.95:1 on plaster, so the product layer sets `--seed-ok: oklch(.52 .1 150)` | Derive the status hues at a lightness that meets contrast, whatever the accent's |
| 2026-11-19 | 0.10 | A dark band (`.section.cc-band` that is a scope with a mood) painted with the scope's own ground | The band's border image reads `--band-bg`, which defaults to `--bg-sunken` whatever the scope, so every dark band came out sunken; `site.css` sets `.section.cc-band[data-theme] { --band-bg: var(--surface) }` | Default `--band-bg` to `--surface` on a band that is a scope with `data-theme` |
| 2026-11-19 | 0.10 | Gold for small words (a role, a year, a kicker), at 4.5:1 on every ground | The accent is only promised 3:1, so `.hw-gold` is `color-mix(in oklab, var(--accent) 60%, var(--text))` (6:1 to 13:1 in the specimen, light and dark, on bands and cards) | An `--accent-text` role: the accent as ink, held to 4.5:1 by the contract test |
| 2026-11-19 | 0.10 | A taller band (the old page heroes, threshold and mission had more room) | `.section.cc-band` pads `--sp-6` with no variable; kept it rather than redeclare `padding-block` | A `--band-pad` variable on `.cc-band` |
| 2026-11-19 | 0.10 | A part whose height is whole cells from its own width (an arch, a roundel, a player, line art) | `round(nearest, calc(100cqi * ratio), var(--u))`, so each must sit in a container (a `.card`, a `.context`, or an `hw-` composition that is one); the specimen wraps them in `.context` | A ratio primitive (`.ratio` with `--ratio`) that is its own container's child, or `aspect-ratio` rounded to cells |
| 2026-11-19 | 0.10 | A `.rail` whose height stays on the cells | On Windows Chromium a thin classic scrollbar adds about 10px under the rail's cell of padding, so the band after the values rail is off its line by that much; left as is (the scrollbar is the only sign it scrolls) | Keep the scrollbar inside the rail's last cell (pad by the cell less the scrollbar, or `scrollbar-gutter`), as the board does for its line |
| 2026-11-19 | 0.10 | Calm motion longer than 250ms: a reveal (the old site's 900ms) and the live dot's pulse (2.4s) | `calc(var(--dur-in-slow) * 3)` and `* 10`; both still collapse under reduced motion | A slow and an ambient duration in the `--dur-*` set |
| 2026-11-19 | 0.10 | An accordion outside running text (the old `.accordion`) | Trellis styles `details`/`summary` only inside `.prose`, so it maps to `.prose > details` (one open at a time with `<details name>`); the homepage hallways became always-open cards, as the draft did | Style a bare `details` (or `details.disclosure`) outside `.prose` with the same parts |
| 2026-11-19 | 0.10 | Two halves of a `.split` that meet on their floor or middle (page hero, the draft's `.hw-facing`) | `.split` declares `align-items: start` with no variable, so the halves take `align-self` instead (`.hw-hero-text`, `.hw-hero-media`) | A `--split-align` variable |
| 2026-11-19 | 0.10 | A button the width of its column (the old `.btn--block`: Notify me, Join, Give) | Nothing: core sets a button's width to its words (`calc-size(fit-content …)`), even directly in a `.stack`, and stretching it would restyle `.button`. Those buttons are now their natural width | `.button.cc-block` (a full-width variant) |
| 2026-11-19 | 0.10 | A button whose words the Site Editor can change (`data-edit="link"` allows no span inside) | The marked `<a class="button">` holds its words directly, without `.button-label`; it centers, but its label isn't trimmed to the capitals | Trim a button's own text when it has no `.button-label` (e.g. `text-box` on `.button` itself when it holds only text) |
